Shops using the app store: staff names, roles and PINs; product, stock and price records; sales, refunds, supplier and customer-credit records; and shop settings. 3. How we use it

Only to run the app for your shop: sign-in, sync across your devices, reports, and support. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

4. Sharing

Data is stored with our infrastructure provider (Supabase) and is visible only to the shop it belongs to, and to our platform administrators for support and licence management. We disclose data if required by Kenyan law.

5. Security

Access is controlled by staff PINs and optional biometric sign-in. Shops should keep PINs private and deactivate staff who leave.
