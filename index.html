Add staff

Each person needs their own PIN - checked as unique across every shop on the platform, not just this one. Give someone their PIN and they can sign in with it on their own phone, anywhere, and the app finds this shop automatically.

Roles, in short

Cashier - sells only, 4-digit PIN. Removing a line or clearing a cart is never blocked - the sale keeps moving - but it's sent to Admin → Removals for an admin or supervisor to review afterward.
Supervisor - everything a cashier can do, plus can remove items or clear a cart immediately, no review needed, still fully logged.
Admin - full access: Stock, Reports, Sync setup, and assigning everyone else's role. Admin PINs are 6 digits - longer on purpose, so it's harder for a cashier to glance at the keypad and pick it up.

Staff list

Role is a dropdown you can change any time - promote a cashier to supervisor without deleting their account. Revoke turns off someone's PIN without deleting their sales history. You can have as many active cashiers as you need.

Online / offline behavior

      
This isn't a toggle you manage day to day - it's automatic.

      

        The app watches the device's real network state. Lose signal mid-sale and nothing changes for the
        cashier - sales keep ringing up, stock keeps deducting, everything queues locally. The moment the
        connection is back, queued sales and stock updates drain to the backend on their own. The button below
        is only there to test that flow on demand, without physically switching off wifi.
        


        One thing to set up once you host this for real: the page itself needs to survive a reload with zero
        network too - that's what manifest.json
        and sw.js (included alongside
        this file) handle. Host all three together and open the app once while online - after that, it opens
        offline, cold, with no connection at all.
